WHO WE ARE

More than just console repairs.

Console Refurb was built around a passion for technology, electronics and finding solutions to problems that others might consider beyond repair.

What started with repairing and refurbishing gaming consoles has developed into a wider technology venture, bringing together electronics repair, software development and website design.

Our aim is simple: provide practical, reliable services while developing products and solutions that make technology easier to use and maintain.

OUR APPROACH

Understanding the problem matters.

We believe good technical work starts with understanding the problem rather than simply replacing parts and hoping for the best.

When working on electronics, we focus on diagnosis, identifying the underlying fault and carrying out appropriate repairs.

Refurbished equipment is approached with the same attention to detail, with inspection and functional testing forming an important part of the process.

We also believe technology should be useful. That's why our software and website projects focus on practical features, straightforward experiences and solving genuine problems.
