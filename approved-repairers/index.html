Our planned approach

How the network will work

We're developing a structured approach to connecting customers with suitable repair specialists.

01. Customer request

A customer submits a console repair request through the Console Refurb website.

02. Repair allocation

The request can be assessed and directed to a suitable participating repair business based on the nature of the fault and availability.

03. Diagnosis & quotation

The repairer assesses the console, records diagnostic findings and provides a repair quotation for customer approval.

04. Repair & completion

Once approved, the repair is completed and the customer is kept informed throughout the process.
