Privacy Policy

How Console Refurb collects, uses and protects personal information.

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

Console Refurb is a trading name used by , a sole trader based in England and Wales. “Console Refurb”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to that business. We are responsible for deciding how and why the personal information described in this policy is used.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may handle:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number and postal address.
  • Information about a console, repair, product, website project or software enquiry.
  • Order, payment, delivery, repair and customer-service information.
  • Newsletter email address, marketing consent, consent timestamp, privacy policy version and registration source.
  • Messages, photographs and other information you choose to send us.
  • Technical information processed by hosting and security providers, such as IP address, browser and request information.

Please do not send payment card details or highly sensitive information by ordinary email.

3. Website hosting and security

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, including IP address and request information, to deliver, maintain and secure the website. Its own terms and privacy information also apply.

The newsletter feature uses Firebase services, including Cloud Firestore, to store registration records. Firebase App Check with reCAPTCHA Enterprise is used to help protect the registration feature against automated submissions and abuse. These services may process technical information needed for security and operation.

We do not currently describe this website as using advertising cookies or behavioural advertising. If we introduce analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking, we will update this policy and provide any consent controls required by law.

4. Contact and service enquiries

If you contact us by email or another contact route, we process the information you provide to respond, provide quotations, discuss or deliver requested services, manage customer relationships, keep appropriate records and deal with complaints or disputes. The website currently directs visitors to email us; it does not currently submit general contact-form details to a database.

5. Newsletter and marketing

If you join the Console Refurb mailing list, we collect your email address and record your consent to receive marketing emails and updates. The registration record also includes a consent timestamp, privacy policy version and a source value identifying the website registration.

Newsletter records are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore. The email address is normalised and a SHA-256 hash is used as the document identifier. The email address itself is also stored in the record; hashing the identifier does not make the email address anonymous.

We intend to use the mailing list for the updates described at registration, such as software releases, development news, testing opportunities, console refurbishment news and relevant offers. We will not use it for unrelated purposes.

You can withdraw consent at any time by emailing consolerefurb@outlook.com with the subject “Newsletter unsubscribe” and identifying the address you used to subscribe. For the initial release, removal is handled manually: we will locate and delete the relevant newsletter record. We aim to action valid requests promptly.

We will not send marketing campaigns until we have a working process for honouring unsubscribe requests. We may retain a minimal record where reasonably necessary to demonstrate or respect a suppression request, where lawful.

6. Repairs, purchases and delivery

If you ask us to repair a console or buy a product, we may process information needed to assess the enquiry, provide a quote, arrange work or sale, take payment through the selected method, arrange collection or delivery, provide after-sales support and deal with warranty claims. This may include contact details, device or product details, fault descriptions, diagnostic findings, photographs taken by the repair business, order details, payment status and communications.

If another independent repairer or service provider is involved, we will explain relevant arrangements and information sharing when the service is arranged.

7. Invoice Maker and other software

Invoice Maker: the application is designed as a local-first app. Invoice and business information you enter, such as business details, customer details, invoice items, prices and notes, is stored locally on your device. We do not routinely receive invoice contents in a cloud database operated by us.

Invoice Maker can generate PDFs on your device. If you print or share a PDF, it is passed to the operating system and service you select; that service's privacy terms then apply.

Invoice Maker Pro purchase and entitlement handling uses RevenueCat and the relevant app store, such as Google Play or Apple's App Store. Those providers may process purchase-related information and an app user identifier. Payment card details are handled by the app store or payment provider, not stored directly by us through Invoice Maker.

FlipSmart and Repair Platform: these products are under development and testing. Their final data handling arrangements will depend on the features and services made available. We will provide or update relevant privacy information before collecting personal information through released versions.

8. How and why we use information

Depending on the circumstances, we use personal information to respond to enquiries; provide repairs, products and other services; manage orders, payments, delivery, collection, returns and warranty matters; send marketing where you have given the required consent; operate and secure our website and software; provide support; keep business, tax and accounting records; and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

9. Lawful bases

We rely on one or more lawful bases under data protection law, depending on the purpose:

  • Contract: to take steps at your request before entering a contract or perform a contract with you.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with legal requirements, including relevant accounting and tax obligations.
  • Consent: for specific uses such as email marketing. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: for appropriate business purposes such as responding to general enquiries, maintaining website security, improving services and keeping records, where your rights do not override those interests.

10. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information. Where needed, we may share relevant information with service providers who help operate the business, including:

  • GitHub Pages and website infrastructure providers.
  • Firebase and Google services for database hosting, application security and abuse prevention.
  • RevenueCat and app-store providers for Invoice Maker purchase and entitlement handling.
  • Payment providers, delivery companies and suppliers needed to complete a transaction or service.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, regulators, law enforcement or other authorities where necessary or required by law.

Third-party providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

11. International transfers

Some providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where UK data protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we will take steps to ensure an appropriate transfer mechanism or safeguard is in place.

12. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was collected, including to meet legal, accounting, tax, warranty and dispute-resolution requirements.

  • Enquiry and customer records are retained as needed to respond, provide services and maintain appropriate business records.
  • Transaction and accounting records may need to be retained for periods required by law.
  • Newsletter records are kept while you remain subscribed. Following an unsubscribe request, the active registration record will be deleted, subject to any limited record reasonably needed to demonstrate or respect the request.
  • Invoice Maker information generally remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data or uninstall the application.

13. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, and to object to certain processing. You can withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You also have an absolute right to object to direct marketing. Some rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions. Contact us below to make a request; we may need to verify your identity.

14. Children's information

Our website and business services are not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information through the website. Please contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information to us.

15. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information under our control. No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. You should also protect your email account, devices and information you choose to send us.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the business, website, software, providers or legal requirements. The current version will be published here with an updated date. If a change materially affects information already collected, we will take any additional steps required by law.

17. Complaints

Please contact us first if you have a concern about how we handle your information. You also have the right to complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
