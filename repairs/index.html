How our repair process works

01. Submit your repair

Tell us which console you have and describe the problem you're experiencing.

02. Initial assessment

We'll review the information and provide an initial estimate where possible.

03. Diagnosis & approval

Once the console has been assessed, we'll confirm the diagnosis and any revised repair cost for approval. We can also share photographs to help explain identified faults.

04. Repair & collection

Following approval, the repair can proceed. We'll update you when your console is ready.
