OUR REFURBISHMENT PROCESS

We believe in doing things properly.

01. Inspection

Consoles are inspected to identify visible damage, faults and any areas requiring attention.

02. Repair

Where necessary, repairs are carried out to address identified hardware or software faults.

03. Testing

Consoles undergo functional checks to assess their operation before being offered for sale.

04. Ready for Sale

Each listing will explain the console's condition, included accessories and applicable warranty.

BUY WITH CONFIDENCE

12-month refurbished console warranty.

We want you to have confidence in your purchase. Qualifying refurbished consoles sold by Console Refurb include a 12-month voluntary warranty covering qualifying hardware faults during normal use.

If a qualifying fault develops, we'll assess the issue and determine the appropriate remedy, which may include repair or replacement depending on the circumstances and your statutory rights.

Our voluntary warranty is additional to your statutory rights under UK consumer law.
