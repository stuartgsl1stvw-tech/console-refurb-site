WHAT IS FLIPSMART?

Everything in one place.

Managing a refurbishment business can involve keeping track of purchases, stock, components, repairs, listings and sales. FlipSmart is being developed to bring these activities together in one organised system.

APPLICATION FEATURES

Designed around the way refurbishment businesses work.

🛒

Purchasing Management

Record purchases and keep track of the equipment entering your business.

📦

Inventory Management

Keep an organised record of your stock, equipment and refurbishment inventory.

🔧

Refurbishment Tracking

Record refurbishment activities and follow the progress of equipment through your workflow.

🔩

Component Management

Keep track of components and parts used during refurbishment work.

🏷️

Marketplace Listings

Manage product listing information and prepare refurbished equipment for sale.

💰

Sales & Financial Reporting

Monitor sales and financial performance with reporting and export capabilities.

BUILT BY CONSOLE REFURB

Developed from real-world experience.

FlipSmart isn't being developed around an imaginary business. It is being developed through our own experience purchasing, repairing, refurbishing and selling gaming consoles.

We understand the importance of keeping track of purchases, parts, refurbishment costs, stock and sales.

Our aim is to create software that addresses these everyday challenges and makes managing a refurbishment business simpler.

THE BIGGER PICTURE

Part of a connected business ecosystem.

FlipSmart is being developed as an independent application, with the potential to connect with other business software.

Our longer-term vision is to connect inventory, repair management, sales and invoicing through a central Business Hub.

Console Refurb will provide a real-world environment in which to demonstrate these connected solutions.
