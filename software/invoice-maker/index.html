WHAT IS INVOICE MAKER?

Invoicing made straightforward.

Creating invoices shouldn't require complicated software or unnecessary business management features. Invoice Maker focuses on the essentials, providing a simple way to enter customer details, add invoice items and generate professional PDF invoices.

BUILT BY CONSOLE REFURB

Practical software for everyday needs.

Invoice Maker is another example of our approach to software development. Rather than building an unnecessarily complicated business application, we've focused on a specific everyday requirement: creating and producing invoices.

The application is being developed using a lightweight approach, with locally stored information and PDF generation at its core.

Our aim is to make invoicing accessible and straightforward for people who simply need a reliable way to produce professional invoices.

PART OF A WIDER ECOSYSTEM

Independent products. Connected possibilities.

Invoice Maker is being developed as an independent application. Our longer-term vision is to connect invoicing with other business systems through our planned Business Hub.

This could allow businesses to generate invoices as part of wider workflows involving sales, inventory and repair management.
