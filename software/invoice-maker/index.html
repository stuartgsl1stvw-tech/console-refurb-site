Professional invoicing without the unnecessary complexity. Invoice Maker is a simple invoicing application designed to help individuals and small businesses create, manage and produce professional PDF invoices. Now available for Windows and Android.

WHAT IS INVOICE MAKER?

Invoicing made straightforward.

Creating invoices shouldn't require complicated software or unnecessary business management features. Invoice Maker focuses on the essentials, providing a simple way to enter customer details, add invoice items, manage your invoices and generate professional PDF documents.

APPLICATION FEATURES

Everything you need to create an invoice.

🧾

Unlimited Invoices

Create and manage as many invoices as you need without subscription limits.

👤

Customer Details

Enter customer information and include the relevant details within your invoices.

📋

Invoice Items

Add individual items, quantities and prices to build your invoices.

📄

PDF Generation

Generate professional PDF invoices that can be previewed, printed and shared with customers.

💾

Local Data Storage

Invoice information and business details are stored locally on your device. No cloud account is required.

Cross-Platform

BUILT BY CONSOLE REFURB

Practical software for everyday needs.

Invoice Maker is another example of our approach to software development. Rather than building an unnecessarily complicated business application, we've focused on a specific everyday requirement: creating and producing invoices.

The application uses locally stored information and PDF generation at its core, providing a straightforward experience without requiring an online account.

Our aim is to make invoicing accessible for people who simply need a reliable way to produce professional invoices.
