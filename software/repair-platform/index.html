WHAT IS REPAIR PLATFORM?

One connected repair journey.

Arranging a repair shouldn't be complicated. Customers need a straightforward way to explain their problem, understand the estimated cost and follow the progress of their repair. Repair businesses need an organised way to manage incoming work, diagnose faults, communicate with customers and complete repairs.

Repair Platform is designed to bring these activities together in one system.

FOR CUSTOMERS

A simpler way to arrange a repair.

Give customers visibility and involvement throughout the repair process.

HOW IT WORKS

From initial enquiry to completed repair.

Repair Platform is designed around a straightforward repair workflow.

01. Customer Enquiry

The customer submits a repair request, describes the fault and provides relevant information about their equipment.

02. Diagnosis & Estimate

The repair business reviews the request, investigates the fault and provides a proposed repair cost.

03. Customer Approval

The customer reviews the proposed repair and decides whether to proceed.

04. Repair & Completion

The business carries out the work, updates the repair status and completes the repair process.

BUILT BY CONSOLE REFURB

Our longer-term vision is to connect repair management with inventory, refurbishment and invoicing through a central Business Hub.

This could allow businesses to manage more of their operations through connected applications while retaining the flexibility to use individual products.
