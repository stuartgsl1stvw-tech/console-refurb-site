90-day protection

Console repair warranty

We stand behind the repair work we carry out. Our 90-day repair warranty covers qualifying issues relating to the work performed.

What is covered?

The repair warranty applies to the specific repair work carried out by Console Refurb. It includes qualifying failures relating to:

  • The repair work performed.
  • Replacement components supplied and fitted as part of the repair.
  • A recurrence of the original fault where it is attributable to the repair.

What isn't covered?

The voluntary repair warranty does not cover unrelated faults or damage arising from:

  • Accidental or physical damage after the repair.
  • Liquid damage or exposure to moisture after the repair.
  • Misuse, neglect or improper handling.
  • Unauthorised modifications or subsequent repair attempts.
  • New faults unrelated to the original repair work.

These exclusions do not affect any rights you may have where the original repair service was not carried out with reasonable care and skill.

12-month protection

Refurbished console warranty

Every qualifying refurbished console sold by Console Refurb comes with a 12-month voluntary warranty for additional peace of mind.

What is covered?

The warranty covers qualifying hardware faults that develop during normal use, including:

  • Internal hardware component failures.
  • Qualifying HDMI and display-related faults.
  • Power-related hardware failures.
  • Storage failures, where applicable.
  • Other qualifying internal hardware faults.

What isn't covered?

The voluntary warranty does not cover faults or damage caused by:

  • Accidental or physical damage.
  • Liquid damage or exposure to moisture.
  • Misuse, neglect or improper handling.
  • Unauthorised modifications or repair attempts.
  • Damage caused by unsuitable accessories or power supplies.

What happens if a fault develops?

If you experience a problem during the warranty period, contact us with your purchase details and a description of the fault.

We will assess the reported issue and determine the appropriate next steps. Depending on the circumstances and your statutory rights, this may include a repair or replacement.

Any remedy will be considered in accordance with applicable consumer law.

Need assistance?

Making a warranty claim

If you believe your repair or refurbished console has developed a fault covered by our warranty, please get in touch.

To help us investigate, please provide:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Your repair reference or purchase details, if available.
  • A description of the problem.
  • Photographs or videos where they may help explain the fault.

We will review the information and advise you on the next steps.
